ecosystem research: institutional saas, refi, and cosmos infrastructure

2025 · research

i mapped a set of projects across education technology, board governance, regenerative finance, and cosmos-based blockchain infrastructure — turning scattered information from websites, docs, repositories, and ecosystem sources into a clear analysis of what each project does, who it serves, how it works, and where it overlaps with adjacent work in coordination, governance, ecological assets, and public goods.

scope

  • regen networkcosmos-based blockchain, registry, and marketplace for ecological credits and regenerative finance.
  • cosmos sdkopen-source framework for building modular, application-specific blockchains.

themes

  • institutional coordination. edoo and exeboard solve coordination problems for traditional institutions — schools and boards.
  • governance systems. exeboard handles formal board governance; dao dao and adjacent tools represent on-chain governance patterns.
  • regenerative finance. regen network, open forest protocol, toucan, and klimadao focus on ecological credits, carbon markets, mrv, and environmental assets.
  • appchain infrastructure. cosmos sdk lets projects like regen network run their own chain — custom logic, governance, validators, interoperability.
  • public-goods funding. gitcoin, giveth, karma, hypercerts, and open source observer help track, fund, and evaluate impact-oriented work.
  • verifiable claims. cheqd, ixo, hypercerts, and regen all connect to the broader need for trusted data, credentials, attestations, and impact records.

main insight — across very different domains, the common problem is coordination. schools, boards, daos, ecological-credit markets, and public-goods funders all need trusted records, clear governance, accountability, and reliable reporting. traditional saas usually coordinates within institutions; web3 projects often coordinate across open networks, communities, and markets. they're approaching the same problems from different directions.
